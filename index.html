The Idea

Every website starts the same way. A blank file, a blinking cursor, and two words that have opened programs since 1972. We decided to stop hiding that moment behind a homepage full of stock photos and service lists nobody reads.

Hello World is the test page taken seriously. No slideshow, no chat widget, no seventeen navigation items. One statement, set big enough to read from across the room, because a business that can say what it is in two words doesn't need to shout the rest.

This page is proof of concept and business card at once. If you build sites, run a shop, or just wanted to see what "hello world" looks like when someone commits to it fully, you're standing in the right place.
